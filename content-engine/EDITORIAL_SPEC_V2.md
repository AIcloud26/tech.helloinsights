# HelloInsights Technology Content Engine V2

## Editorial objective
Publish useful technology journalism and analysis that reads like edited human work, not a generic AI summary.

## Required research layer
Every article should be grounded in identifiable facts: company/product names, dates, releases, specifications, prices where relevant, metrics, source links, or concrete examples. Never invent sources, quotes, statistics, users, tests, or expert opinions.

## Article angle
Before drafting, define one clear angle:
- What changed?
- Why does it matter now?
- Who is affected?
- What is different from the previous situation?
- What should the reader watch next?

## Writing rules
- Lead with the most useful fact, not a generic introduction.
- Prefer concrete nouns, verbs, dates and numbers.
- Mix short and long sentences naturally.
- Avoid repetitive section patterns.
- Do not use filler transitions such as “In today's rapidly evolving landscape”, “It is worth noting”, “Looking ahead”, or “In conclusion”.
- Do not manufacture a “pros and cons” section unless the subject genuinely calls for it.
- Do not repeat the headline in the opening paragraph.
- Use subheads only when they improve scanning.
- Add disagreement, limitations, trade-offs, or uncertainty when supported by the evidence.
- Separate reported facts from HelloInsights analysis.
- Do not claim first-hand testing unless a real test was performed.

## Recommended structures

### News
Lead → what changed → context → impact → what happens next

### Analysis
Thesis → evidence → context → competing interpretation → implications → HelloInsights view

### Explainer
Definition → how it works → practical example → limitations → who should care

### Product / platform
What changed → real-world use → strengths → constraints → who benefits → verdict

## Originality layer
Each article should contain at least one specific editorial contribution: comparison, interpretation, implication, counterpoint, scenario, or practical takeaway. Do not add generic “our take” paragraphs to every story.

## Final quality gate
Reject or rewrite if:
- the first paragraph could fit 20 unrelated technology stories;
- more than two consecutive paragraphs use the same sentence rhythm;
- claims have no identifiable factual basis;
- the article only paraphrases a press release;
- the conclusion merely repeats the introduction;
- the text contains obvious AI filler or template phrases.
